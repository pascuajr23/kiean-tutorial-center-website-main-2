How booking details are used

KieAn uses the parent’s contact details, learner’s first name, grade and learning request to arrange these lessons. Booking records are stored on the center’s booking server, and scheduling details are shared with the assigned teacher through Google Calendar. No payment details or medical documents are collected. Contact the center through its Facebook page to request a correction or deletion.
