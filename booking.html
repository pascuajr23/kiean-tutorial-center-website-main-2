    How booking details are used

    What we collect

    • Your name, email address and contact number.
    • Your learner’s first name, grade and learning request, plus any homework or worksheets you attach.
    • Your area (city or town), program, branch or online format, teacher, dates and time.
    • A payment receipt, if you upload one.

    Why we use it

    Only to schedule and run these lessons, confirm your payment, and contact you about your booking. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

    Who sees it

    • Your assigned teacher and KieAn staff, through the teacher’s Google Calendar and a booking email that includes your files and receipt.
    • Google, which runs the calendars, Google Meet and the emails we send.
    • OpenStreetMap, only if you tap “Use my location”: your browser’s coordinates are sent once to find your town and are not stored by KieAn.

    What you receive

    Every booking emails you the full schedule. Online bookings also send one Google Calendar invitation from KieAn with a Google Meet link.

    How long we keep it

    Booking records, files and receipts are kept on KieAn’s booking server for as long as the center needs them for lessons and records.

    Your choices

    You may ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time, as provided by the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. Read the full Privacy Policy. Message KieAn on Facebook and quote your booking reference.
