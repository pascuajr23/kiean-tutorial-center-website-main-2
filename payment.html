How to Pay with GCash

After you book, KieAn sends you a confirmation email with your booking reference (for example, KTC-12345678) and your total. Pay that total by GCash, then reply to that same email with your receipt. Please pay before your first session.

  1. Pay the total from your booking email by GCash.
  2. Screenshot the GCash receipt.
  3. Reply to your booking email and attach the screenshot.

1. Scan our GCash QR code

Before you confirm a payment, check that GCash shows the account name above. Until the QR code is posted here, please pay at the branch or message us on Facebook for payment details.

2. Pay in the GCash app

  1. Open GCash and tap QR. Scan the code above, or save it to your phone and tap Upload QR.
  2. Enter the exact total shown in your booking email.
  3. In the message field, type your booking reference and your child’s first name, for example “KTC-12345678 Elle”.
  4. Tap Pay, then take a screenshot of the receipt. It shows the amount, date and GCash reference number.

3. Send us your receipt

  • Open the booking confirmation email you received from KieAn and tap Reply. Its subject starts with your first session date and ends with your booking reference.
  • Attach the receipt screenshot and send. Keep the booking reference in the email so we can match your payment.
  • Already uploaded a receipt while booking? You do not need to send it again.

4. What happens next

  • The center checks every payment against your booking and replies to confirm it.
  • Your teacher sees the booking as paid once the payment is confirmed.
  • An official receipt is issued as required by the Bureau of Internal Revenue.

Other ways to pay

You may also pay in person at your branch. Bring your booking reference.

Problems or questions

  • Paid the wrong amount, paid twice, or can’t find your booking email? Message KieAn on Facebook with your booking reference.
  • KieAn will never ask for your GCash MPIN or one-time code (OTP). Never share them with anyone.
  • Need to cancel or reschedule? See our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
