Cookie Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. KieAn does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and parents booking lessons receive no cookies from us at all. That is why this website does not show a cookie banner.

1. Essential cookies (staff only)

CookiePurposeDuration
kiean_adminKeeps KieAn staff signed in to the admin area.8 hours, or until sign-out
kiean_oauth_stateProtects the step where staff connect a Google Calendar.10 minutes

These cookies are strictly necessary, are set only for staff, and are never used to track visitors.

2. Third-party services

  • OpenStreetMap: contacted only if you tap “Use my location” on the booking page.
  • Facebook: our Messenger and Facebook links open Facebook, where Meta’s own cookies and policies apply.

3. Other browser storage

The website does not save anything else in your browser’s storage.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies does not affect booking; staff need cookies enabled to use the admin area.

5. Contact us

See our Privacy Policy, or message KieAn on Facebook.
